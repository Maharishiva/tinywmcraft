the rules

Beat the best score in one 600-second A100 run.

data
train.npz on Hugging Face, 500k transitions. Nothing else, no pretrained weights.
compute
600 s on one A100 40GB, everything included: tokenizer, data loading, compilation. No compile cache.
eval
eval.py is frozen. Loading your model, compiling and generating every clip must take at most 300 s on the maintainer's Colab A100 (iteration 3: about 170 s). Any model, any sampler, any context length.
frames
One frame per action: the model never sees future actions or frames.
seeds
The score is the mean over training seeds 0, 1 and 2.
gates
Ranked on the score alone, checked in the maintainer's rerun on the same machine and seeds, on a private test set built the same way from other worlds.

The score must beat the best mean by a noise-aware margin, where sd is the sample SD over the three seeds:

new − best > 1.65 · √((sd_best² + sd_new²) / 3)

Reward AP must stay at or above a fixed floor, 95% of iteration 1: reward AP ≥ 0.877. The floor does not move when the best changes. Done AP is reported, not gated.

evaluation

The frozen evaluator scores clips of held-out play from other worlds. Eight real frames prime the model, then it generates one frame per recorded action. Probes read the generated frames with the game's own textures: HUD digits, map cells, the sleep tint. The eval takes about 3 minutes on one A100.

score ↑
The headline: the mean of four parts, each clipped to [0, 1]: gFVD on a log scale from copying the last frame (170, scores 0) to the real game replayed with fresh randomness (14, scores 1); AF from blind (0.5) to the replayed game (0.93); logic; memory. Copying the last frame, shuffling its tiles or ignoring the actions all score 0.
logic ↑
Eight scripted actions from a real state next to something to use: chop, mine, drink, eat, place, craft, sleep, with and without the right tool, table, furnace or ingredients, crafts in any order. Per family, the pass rate where the game changed something plus the pass rate where it did not, minus 1.
memory ↑
Walks out and back: map cells seen before come back into view. Recall is scored as a gain over drawing a random block of the last context frame, in gap buckets from 4-7 to 64+ frames. Every model so far is at chance past 16 frames.
gFVD ↓ (×10⁴)
The Fréchet distance between real and generated clips over all 64 generated frames, in the feature space of a fixed random CNN. It needs no external checkpoint and is not comparable to I3D FVD.
AF ↑
Action following: the share of clips where the true actions predict the salient pixels better than random actions, under the same context and noise. A model that ignores the actions scores 0.5.
reward AP ↑ and done AP ↑
The average precision of the reward and episode-end heads on real transitions.

how to submit

  1. Train seeds 0, 1 and 2 and score each with eval.py. Every run appends a row to ledger.jsonl.
  2. Open a pull request with your code, the three ledger rows and links to the three checkpoints on Hugging Face, so anyone can play and score them.
  3. The maintainer retrains the three seeds next to the best on a Colab A100. If the rerun passes the gates, it is the next iteration: the crown goes next to your GitHub login, and your model becomes the one the site plays. If it does not export to the browser with docs/export_web.py, the maintainer helps.
